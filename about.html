The Story Behind AdBlinko

I Failed NEET Twice.

I was sitting at my desk staring at the wall. The exam was in two months. This was my second attempt at NEET, and I had already decided something: This would be my last.

I had spent entire days studying chapters like electrostatics. I watched the lectures, I wrote down every formula, and I solved the PYQs over and over until I felt satisfied. But then, on May 4th, sitting in the exam hall, I looked at a question from the exact chapter I had poured my soul into.

It felt familiar, like seeing someone's face but not remembering their name. I sat there trying to force my brain to remember, but nothing came. And slowly, with every question I couldn't solve, my confidence faded.

"I didn't fail because I didn't study hard. I failed because I didn't know how to learn. That realization changed everything."

The Search for the Truth

I was so frustrated that all those early mornings, late nights, and dozens of books filled with notes had amounted to nothing. I decided I needed to understand why I failed. What were the toppers doing differently?

I spent 4 months searching. I read books, articles, research papers on cognitive science, and studied memory systems. Very soon, the answers became clear. The things we consider "right" — reading, re-reading, and passively watching lectures — were actually the worst ways to study.

Why I Created NEET Mastery

I wished someone had taught me the right way to study before I ever picked up a biology book. I realized that millions of other students are making the exact same mistakes I did, burning themselves out while using study methods that guarantee they will blank out in the exam hall.

That is why AdBlinko exists, and it's why I wrote NEET Mastery. It is a 60-page guide detailing the science-backed system for focus, memory, and scoring higher. No fluff. No false motivation. Just the raw cognitive science of how to actually learn, retain, and recall information.

Stop studying harder. Start studying right.
